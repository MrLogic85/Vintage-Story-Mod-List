# Companions

**Mods:** Pet AI, Wolf taming, Pet Map Markers, BedSpawn

## Pet AI

The foundation mod for everything else here — a library of taming/companion AI behaviors that **Wolf taming** and **Pet Map Markers** both require to function. Its own docs say most other mods should peacefully coexist with it; it's a well-behaved dependency.

## Wolf taming

Does exactly what it says — turns wolves into tameable, long-term companions instead of a threat or a hide source. Requires Pet AI.

## Pet Map Markers

Adds waypoints for your tamed pets so you can find them after they wander off. Fully tested and supported alongside both Pet AI and Wolf taming. One real interaction worth knowing about: it has a **partial incompatibility with Cartographer** (see [Exploration & World](exploration-and-world.md)) — you can run both together fine, but *sharing* pet waypoints through Cartographer's sharing feature is explicitly called out as causing "Weird Stuff." Keep pet waypoints local and you're fine.

## BedSpawn

Sets your respawn point when you sleep in a bed, rather than leaving you locked to your original world spawn. Not mechanically tied to the pet/companion mods above, but grouped here as part of "things that make your home base matter more."

## How to actually use it

Tame wolves early if you want backup on hunts — the investment pays off fast with Pet AI doing the heavy lifting. Use Pet Map Markers freely for your own tracking, but don't rely on sharing pet waypoints with other players through Cartographer. Sleep in a bed once you've got a base worth respawning at.
