# Danger & Wildlife

**Mods:** Temporals Reformed, BloodTrail

## Temporals Reformed: enemies with actual behavior

This isn't just a stat multiplier — each temporal creature type behaves differently, and the behavior changes with tier:

- **Drifters**: low tiers are territorial and cautious, only attacking if you enter their space; high tiers are feral and aggressive. They punch with their left hand, charge at close range, and their rock-throwing accuracy and frequency have both been reduced. Depending on tier, they may flee when hurt.
- **Shivers**: erratic stalkers that circle you at variable distance while keeping line of sight, mixing bites with punch/swipe combos and lunges. They can climb any surface, the same way locusts do. Low tiers are cautious; the top tier is genuinely dangerous.
- **Bowtorns**: early tiers strategically avoid you; high tiers are reckless hit-and-run chargers with armor-piercing melee and better terrain-climbing at higher stepheight. The top tier has special tactics and forms packs around a higher-tier leader.

System-wide: spawn rate is roughly half of vanilla, and they need more darkness to spawn at all, so they avoid daylight. Fear scales with light brightness — a bright lantern wards them off more effectively than a dim lamp. They'll attack predator-type animals on sight, and dropping meat is a legitimate distraction tactic if you need to escape. Ranged attacks make them flee, take cover, or charge, depending on the creature type. Bells now only summon mechanical enemies and sawblades, on a shorter cooldown than before. Loot tables are unchanged from vanilla, but killing a temporal creature now grants more temporal stability than it used to.

## BloodTrail

Any weapon hit that does more than 1 damage makes an animal start bleeding — a bare-handed kill won't trigger it. The blood is a particle effect: denser when the animal is running than walking, washes away faster in rain, and disappears instantly on contact with water. Drifters and other "lore" creatures don't bleed at all, since thematically they aren't normal flesh. You'll need particles enabled in your graphics settings to see any of this — and there's a client-side toggle even on servers that force blood trails on, if you'd rather not see it.

## Putting it together

Don't treat all temporal creatures the same — a Shiver's climbing and erratic circling calls for different tactics than a Bowtorn pack forming around its leader. Light matters more than it used to: a strong light source is real defense, not just visibility. If you're overwhelmed, dropping meat to distract nearby predator-type animals (which the temporal creatures will attack) is a legitimate escape option. When hunting normally, a solid hit followed by tracking the blood trail beats chasing a wounded animal blind — just make sure particles are on in your settings.
