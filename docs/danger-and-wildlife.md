# Danger & Wildlife

**Mods:** Temporals Reformed, BloodTrail

## Temporals Reformed

Rebalances temporal enemies toward survival-horror: individually far more dangerous (higher health and damage), but roughly half the vanilla spawn rate, with more darkness required to spawn at all. The design goal, in the author's own words, is that *"you won't fight unless you absolutely have to defend yourself"* — enemies react to light and ranged attacks, interact with animals, and get harder to handle as you progress through creature tiers.

The author describes it as **"very experimental and prone to drastic changes"** — expect balance to shift under you across updates. That said, recent releases show active bugfixing (a bell-spawning bug and Shiver hitboxes were both fixed within the last few releases), which is a good sign for a young mod rather than a red flag.

**One real compatibility note:** this is **not recommended alongside Combat Overhaul** (not installed here) — the two clash on mob health/damage rebalancing, and console warnings appear if both run. If Combat Overhaul is ever added later, switch to the separate "Temporals Lite" mod instead of this one (see the repo's `MOD_DB.json` for the tracked comparison).

It only touches vanilla entity data (health, damage, spawn rates, behavior) — no worldgen changes, no custom save data — so it should be safe to add or remove from an existing world, though this isn't explicitly guaranteed by the author.

## BloodTrail

Wounded animals leave a visible blood trail, turning a chase into a trackable hunt. It maintains compatibility patches for over 20 other animal/creature mods, so it plays well with whatever wildlife mods get added later.

## How to actually use it

Play Temporals Reformed's danger seriously — a single temporal enemy is meant to actually threaten you now, not just add to a horde count. Avoid fights you don't need, per its own design intent. BloodTrail rewards a more deliberate hunting style: land a hit, then follow the trail rather than chasing blind.
