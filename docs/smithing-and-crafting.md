# Smithing & Crafting

**Mods:** SmithingPlus (+ Bug Fix), Knapster, QP's Chisel Tools, Stone Quarry (Standalone Repack), Improved Handbook Recipes (Fork)

Everything here deepens a specific stage of the crafting loop rather than adding a new one — smithing gets repair/QoL, knapping gets faster, stonework gets a proper quarry, and the handbook gets easier to actually use once you've got this many new recipes floating around.

## SmithingPlus + SmithingPlus Bug Fix (install as a pair)

SmithingPlus adds tool repair and quality-of-life improvements to smithing and the anvil. It's currently a release candidate (1.9.0-rc.1), and that RC has a real bug: **looking at an anvil for the first time causes a multi-second freeze**, plus a chisel-drag-hold delay and an iron-bloom-heating stutter. **SmithingPlus Bug Fix** exists specifically to patch this — it's version-pinned to SmithingPlus 1.9.0-rc.1 exactly, so if SmithingPlus ever updates, the bug fix mod needs to update (or be dropped) alongside it. Once SmithingPlus ships a stable release that absorbs these fixes itself, the Bug Fix mod becomes removable.

**Don't also install** Thrifty Smithing or Anvil Metal Recovery — SmithingPlus already covers the same tool/bits-recovery ground, and Anvil Metal Recovery is specifically flagged as a lag source when combined with Knapster's Instant Complete feature.

## Knapster

Speeds up the fiddly, repetitive parts of early tool progression (knapping, gathering). Confirmed compatible with SmithingPlus, and has an optional integration with A Culinary Artillery's "Easy Mixing" feature (see [Cooking & Food](cooking-and-food.md)). The author only ships stable-build releases, never pre-releases — so if Vintage Story itself is on a pre-release, don't expect a same-day Knapster update.

## QP's Chisel Tools

Rewards detail work at the chisel — this is the mod for anyone who wants to actually sculpt rather than just place blocks.

## Stone Quarry (Standalone Repack)

Adds a real quarrying loop — plug-and-feather stone extraction, stone slabs, rubble storage. This is a repack of an original "Stone Quarry" mod whose author has stepped back from updating for 1.22; the repack specifically **removed the CommonLib dependency**, which is also why it's unaffected by the CommonLib/CommonLib-Forked situation described in [Foundation](foundation-libraries.md).

## Improved Handbook Recipes (Fork)

Not a crafting mechanic itself — it's what makes the *rest* of this list bearable to actually use. Lets you scroll freely between recipe variants in the handbook, adds auto-fill buttons, and shows which ingredients in a recipe won't get consumed. With this many mods adding recipes, this is the one that keeps the handbook from becoming useless clutter.

## How to actually use it

Lean on Improved Handbook Recipes from the start — it's the tool you'll use to make sense of everything else here. Expect SmithingPlus's anvil freeze on your very first anvil interaction each session; it's real, it's patched, and it's a known one-time hitch, not a sign something's broken. Knapster pays off immediately in the early game; Stone Quarry and QP's Chisel Tools are worth setting up once you've got a base going and want to invest in proper stonework.
