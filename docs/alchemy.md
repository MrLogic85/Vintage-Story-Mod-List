# Alchemy

**Mods:** Alchemy

## Three tiers, three levels of commitment

**Tier 1 — Edible herbs.** Just a mortar & pestle. Grind herbs into edible balls for weak, short versions of the mod's effects — no other infrastructure needed, available from the start of the game.

**Tier 2 — Medium potions.** Needs a brewing cauldron (copper age) and a wooden stirring spoon. Produces proper liquid potions in flasks, sealed in a barrel — noticeably stronger than the tier 1 herb balls.

**Tier 3 — Strong potions.** Needs a glass stirring spoon, aqua vitae, and herbs dried on an herb rack first. The strongest versions of every effect.

## What the potions do

- **Combat/utility flasks** (each in weak/medium/strong versions): Archer's Flask, Fighter's Flask, Hunter's Delight, Looter's Delight, Miner's Flask, Sprinter's Flask, Vitality Flask (raises max health), Healing Oil, Potent Oil, Spiked Oil (poison damage), Predator Pheromones, Scent Mask, Purging Brew, Settling Brew
- **Single-strength utility flasks**: Recall Flask (teleport), Glow Flask, Water Breathing Flask, Warmth Flask, Stability Flask, Nutrition Flask, Featherfall Flask, Spider Flask, Growth/Shrinking Flasks, Reshape Flask, Flight Flask (creative mode only)

Drink a potion directly, coat a weapon with it, or throw the flask for a splash effect on an area. You can't stack a second dose of the same potion's effect while it's already active (configurable if you want to change that).

## Compatibility notes

Potions aren't food — no freshness/perishability mechanic, so they don't interact with FoodShelves (see [Cooking & Food](cooking-and-food.md)) at all. There's a built-in patch for A Culinary Artillery (also installed): with "drink from any container" enabled in the config, you can drink a potion straight from one of ACA's glass bottles instead of needing Alchemy's own flask.

The mod is heavily configurable — hundreds of values covering effect strength, duration, brewing time, and per-potion enable/disable — worth a look if any single effect feels too strong or too weak for your game.

## Putting it together

Start with tier 1 herb balls the moment you've got a mortar & pestle — there's no reason to wait. Move to the cauldron once you're in the copper age for real liquid potions, and save the tier 3 setup (glass spoon, dried herbs) for when you want the strongest versions of a specific effect for something demanding — a boss fight, a long expedition, a deep mine push. If you're already carrying ACA's glass bottles, enable "drink from any container" so you're not juggling a separate flask type just for this.
