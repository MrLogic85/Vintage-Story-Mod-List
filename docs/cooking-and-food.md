# Cooking & Food

**Mods:** A Culinary Artillery, Expanded Foods (Core, Meals Module, Chef's Tricks), FoodShelves, Butchering, Stone Bake Oven, Real Smoke, SpecializedBags Revived, BetterTraders

A full chain from killing/harvesting through processing, cooking, storing, and trading the surplus.

## Butchering: from carcass to meal

This is the mod behind "make a hook to skin, a table to strip the meat." The full workflow:

1. **Field-harvest with a knife** if you're in a hurry — but you only get **50% yield**.
2. **Carry the carcass home** instead (a **Butchering Bag**, 3–6 slots depending on tier, is built for this — otherwise it just eats your backpack slots).
3. **Hang it on a Butchering Hook.** Hooks come in Primitive, Copper, and Metal tiers (80% / 100% / 120% yield) — metal hooks even have ceiling-mounted variants. The carcass bleeds out; put a bucket 3–5 blocks below to catch the blood.
4. **Once it's bled out, move it to a Butchering Table** (same three tiers) and finish the job with a knife or cleaver.

What you get out: red/bush/poultry meat, hides, bones, fat, blood, offal (needs cleaning in a water barrel before use), sinew (needs drying first), prime meat (redmeat animals only), and ivory from boars. From there: blood sausage and black pudding, smoked meats via the **Smoking Rack** (place a campfire underneath it), bone arrows and needles, and hide/sinew glue.

## A Culinary Artillery + Expanded Foods: the actual cooking

A Culinary Artillery is the toolset: bottles, saucepans, mini-cauldrons, mixing bowls (hand-cranked and powered), bottle racks, rolling pins, meat hooks/racks, spiles, clay molds, and shelf blocks. A few specific mechanics worth knowing: right-click an egg over a bowl or bucket to crack it (yolk and white separate, shells become animal feed); spiles tap trees seasonally for saps and liquids; mixing bowls combine a filling + topping + liquid (matched by ingredient type) to make pies.

Expanded Foods is where the actual dishes live, built entirely on those tools — but as of mid-2026 it's split across three mods, all required together: **Core** (base foods and preservation), **Meals Module** (the actual cooked dishes), and **Chef's Tricks** (extra processing). Core covers fruit leather, gozinaki, trail mix, pemmican, yogurt, cheese and cottage cheese, wine and cider (fermented juice), and vinegar (deliberately spoiled alcohol). Meat ages on meat hooks into dried, charred, or sausage forms; milk gets pasteurized then fermented into yogurt or pressed into cheese; nuts, seeds, and legumes get pressed for cooking oil.

**Meals Module** adds the actual meal recipes — dumplings, fried balls, salads, cold pasta, fruit/herbal/berry breads, muffins, au gratin, pilaf, stuffed peppers, and more — all scooped from a cooking pot or mixing bowl rather than crafted directly. **Chef's Tricks** adds the supporting processing chain: salt from saltwater, resin tapping, breadcrumb animal feed from eggshells, eggshell-to-lime, and extra uses for cooking oil (including as a fuel/lubricant for machinery) — several of these feed ingredients back into Meals Module recipes. Cooking methods overall span boiling, simmering, frying, firepit-charring, clay-oven baking, and barrel fermentation/curing.

One thing worth knowing: meals containing bone broth have a documented perish-timer bug that spoils them prematurely — not a big deal, just don't be surprised if one goes off faster than expected.

## Stone Bake Oven

A modular multiblock, not a single crafted item: an **Oven Heat Source** (load it with firewood and light it) connected to **Heat Distribution Blocks** (corner, T-junction, and endcap pieces that carry heat in up to 4 directions) leading to a **Baking Space** (holds 4 small items or 1 pie — an upgraded version of the vanilla clay oven) and/or a **Cooking Space** (holds cooking pots). Comes in plain, cobblestone, and brick variants.

## Real Smoke: build your kitchen with a chimney

Not something you craft directly — it's a physics layer on top of every fire source you already have. Torches, firepits, and pit kilns all emit smoke that rises, spreads, sinks, and blows with the wind (a pit kiln puts out 12 units/sec, six times a torch's 2/sec). **Cook indoors without a chimney and you'll fill the room with smoke.** Porous blocks like ladders, grates, and fences let smoke pass through; thatch slows and cools it; you can chisel custom vents as small as 4×4 voxels if you want precise control.

## SpecializedBags Revived: pick a bag for the job

Six themed bags, each in three tiers (linen base → sturdy → travel, 12/14/18 slots): **Agriculture Bag** (seeds, grain, vegetables, soil, hoes), **Hunter Bag** (bones, hides, pelts, raw meat, arrows), **Forestry Bag** (axes, logs, saplings, resin, vines), **Builder Bag** (bricks, glass, planks, tiles), **Angler Bag** (fish, bait, poles, seashells), and **Looting Bag** (carpets, rugs, tapestries, decor). Each is crafted from a base linen bag plus one thematic ingredient — the Hunter Bag, for instance, needs fat.

## FoodShelves: where it all goes

No crafting workflow of its own — just a large set of storage blocks, each with a different freshness multiplier (0.3x–0.75x normal perish rate): Pie/Cheese Shelf, Egg Shelf, Vegetable/Fruit Basket, Bar Shelf, Seed Shelf/Bins, Cooling Cabinet and Meat Freezer (coolable with ice), Barrel Racks, Tun Racks, a Jar Stand with Jars/Large Jars, a Ceiling Rack, Flour Sack, Mushroom Basket, Fruit Cooler, Wall Cabinets, Double Shelf, Food Display Case, and Pumpkin Case. Match the shelf to the food type for the best freshness.

## BetterTraders

No new trades or mechanics — existing vanilla traders spawn in proper huts, shelters, and outposts (including cold-climate variants) instead of the repetitive vanilla wagon, at the same minimum spacing. This only affects newly-generated chunks, so existing explored areas won't change.

## Putting it together

Hunt or trap an animal, carry it home in a Butchering Bag, hang it on a hook to bleed out, then finish it on a table. Age the meat on A Culinary Artillery's meat hooks or run it into Expanded Foods' recipes. Bake in a Stone Bake Oven, smoke on Butchering's Smoking Rack, and always vent your kitchen — Real Smoke isn't cosmetic. Store the results by type in FoodShelves, carry field materials in the SpecializedBags that match what you're doing, and sell the surplus at a BetterTraders outpost.
