# 2026-09-17

## Added toolsmithweapons (Weaponsmith - a Toolsmith addon) to MOD_DB.json

Self-authored Toolsmith addon, developed and playtested over the course of this session in its
own separate repo (github.com/MrLogic85/Weaponsmith-a-Toolsmith-addon — not this repo), extending
Toolsmith's head/handle/binding tinkering system to the vanilla spear and sword. Published to
mods.vintagestory.at as `toolsmithweapons` (assetid 122956) at v1.0.0, then added here per the
user's request now that it's a real public release rather than a local dev build.

Added a `checked` entry summarizing the four bugs found and fixed along the way (all specific to
spear/sword being the first hold-to-charge/thrown items ever paired with Toolsmith) - full
root-cause writeups and log evidence live in that other repo's `docs/findings.md`, not duplicated
here. Also added a "Weaponsmith" section to `docs/smithing-and-crafting.md` alongside the existing
Toolsmith writeup, and added the addon to that page's top mod list.

Ran `sync-mods.ps1` afterward - it correctly recognized the zip already sitting in `Mods/` (placed
there manually during this session's dev/test work) and adopted it into `.sync-state.json`
without re-downloading anything.

No incompatibilities found or expected - the addon only patches vanilla engine methods and
Toolsmith's own config utilities, nothing that touches any other installed mod.
