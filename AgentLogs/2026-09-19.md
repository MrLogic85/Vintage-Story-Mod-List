## Installed Temporal Stabilizer

Context: user's home is built in a large ruin and is losing temporal stability slowly even indoors
and outdoors alike. Investigated Vintage Story's actual stability mechanic (via the wiki and forum
dev replies, not from memory) — area stability is a fixed value randomized per location at world
generation, doesn't change afterward, and isn't tied to being a ruin, a home, or indoor/outdoor
status (that only matters for temporal-storm protection, a separate mechanic). So the ruin's spot
just has a naturally low baseline; nothing to "fix" architecturally.

Looked at options: the vanilla worldconfig toggle (`temporalStability false`, too blunt — kills the
mechanic entirely) and vanilla Rift Ward (blocks rifts, doesn't touch baseline area stability), the
Chunk Stability Override mod (`/stab X` command, permanently raises a chunk's baseline — arguably
the more surgical fix), and Temporal Stabilizer (a craftable/placeable machine that makes an area
immune to stability drain while fueled with Temporal Gears). User chose to try Temporal Stabilizer.

Added `vcftemporalstabilizer` (modid slug from the API — asset page shows as "show/mod/58281") to
MOD_DB.json at v1.13.1, with a `checked` entry noting: it's a young mod (771 downloads), 1.13.1 just
fixed a crash-on-touch/placement bug and a flashy-UI-text crash, an open bug remains where the
configurable radius resets on chunk/game reload, model/animations are author-acknowledged WIP, and
there's no confirmed report either way on our game version (1.22.7) since it's only tagged for
1.22.3. Side is "both" (needs server-side too) but this setup shares one Mods/ folder for
client+server, so that's already covered.

Ran `sync-mods.ps1` — installed cleanly, 55 mods now installed.
